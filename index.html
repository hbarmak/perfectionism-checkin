AMPERE Interactive Reflection

Adaptive and Maladaptive Perfectionism in Engineering

This exploratory reflection tool translates the AMPERE framework into an interactive browser-based experience inspired by doctoral research conducted by Dr. Haleh Brotherton.

AMPERE examines two dimensions of perfectionism: adaptive perfectionism, associated with achievement striving and high academic standards, and maladaptive perfectionism, associated with evaluative concerns and self-criticism.

These dimensions can coexist. Your responses will be summarized separately to support reflection rather than assign you to a fixed perfectionism category.

The original instrument was developed with engineering undergraduates. The individualized feedback and activities on this website are exploratory educational applications and have not been separately validated.

Privacy: Responses are processed locally in your browser. This page does not transmit or save your questionnaire answers or written reflections.
