About AMPERE

AMPERE (Adaptive and Maladaptive Perfectionism in Engineering) explores how achievement striving and evaluative concerns may shape students' academic experiences.

This research-informed tool invites you to reflect on your academic standards, motivation, and experiences of pressure.

Research Background and Important Information

AMPERE was developed through doctoral research conducted by Dr. Haleh Brotherton with engineering undergraduates.

Adaptive perfectionism refers here to achievement striving and high academic standards. Maladaptive perfectionism refers to evaluative concerns, dissatisfaction, and self-criticism. These dimensions can coexist.

This interactive reflection experience is exploratory and educational. It is not a diagnostic assessment.

The dissertation-derived thresholds used for reflection pathways (adaptive 4.25 and maladaptive 3.36) are sample-specific research values, not validated cutoffs for classifying individuals.

The suggested activities have not been validated as individualized interventions.

Privacy: Questionnaire responses and written reflections are processed locally in your browser. This page does not transmit or automatically save your answers.
