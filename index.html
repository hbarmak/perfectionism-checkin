Adaptive and Maladaptive Perfectionism in Engineering (AMPERE)

This exploratory reflection tool translates the AMPERE framework (Adaptive and Maladaptive Perfectionism in Engineering) into an interactive browser-based experience inspired by doctoral research conducted by Dr. Haleh Brotherton.

AMPERE examines two dimensions of perfectionism: adaptive perfectionism, associated with achievement striving and high academic standards, and maladaptive perfectionism, associated with evaluative concerns and self-criticism.

These dimensions can coexist. Your responses will be summarized separately to support personal reflection rather than to assign you to a fixed perfectionism category.

The instrument was developed in the context of engineering undergraduate education. The feedback provided here is exploratory and has not been independently validated as an individual interpretation system.

Privacy: This standalone version processes your responses locally in your browser. It does not send or save your answers to a server.
